The AI, in plain English

It drafts. A person confirms. Nothing is asserted without sign-off.

instructBrain looks at every photograph, describes what it sees in the language you would use, decides how serious it is, and says what to do about it. It describes conditions and hazards only, never people. When it cannot tell, it says so rather than guessing, and no guess becomes a pass.

The AI drafts, a person issues

Every finding waits for a human. You confirm or correct, then issue. Nothing enters a report without someone putting their name to it.

Not assessed is a real answer

Bad light, an awkward angle, an obscured detail. When the AI cannot tell, the item reads not assessed instead of a guess. No guess becomes a pass.

No people in the record

The AI never describes, counts or identifies people in a photograph. Findings that do involve people are restricted to supervisors and above, and excluded from subcontractor distribution.

Grades are proposed, then confirmed

A condition grade is proposed with a confidence figure and stays to be confirmed until a person accepts it. There is no silent default grade.

Trades are suggestions

A suggested trade is a suggestion, not an assertion. A person confirms every assignment before anything is distributed.

The record is dated and attributable

Each finding carries the photograph it came from, the date, and the person who confirmed it, so the trail stands up later.

Manual reports, no AI in the path

For the jobs where a model must not touch the record.

Sometimes the honest answer to "can we rely on what it says?" is that no AI should be in the report at all. Manual mode is a deliberate setting, not a fallback. The AI is switched off for that report and every item is authored by a person. Nothing in the delivered document was drafted or graded by a model.

  • ✓Its own presentation: two items per A4 page, showing only the stable item reference and the capture date and time.
  • ✓Delivered as a final issued PDF, by design, rather than a recipient review page.
  • ✓Built for insurers, legal and contractual work, and for a client who will not accept an AI drafted finding.

The reports you can issue

Six report types, one evidence discipline.

A report is one job, from upload to issued PDF. Every type below is live in the product today.

Schedule of Condition

Every element graded A to D on visible condition.

Element by element, each one photographed and graded. One grade per element, confirmed by a person. The report closes with a count of items per grade, and carries a mandatory scope and limitations block on the page itself. This is the one large property firms ask for by name.

The snag write-up

One photograph becomes a defect item with a title, the required action, an alternative fix, a trade tip and a health and safety note. One item per photograph, many photographs in one pass.

Property Inventories

A photographic inventory of a property or a space, item by item. Each item is captured with its own defined photo roles and limits, held in the versioned survey definition, so the same template produces a consistent record every time. Used for handover, handback and asset records.

Project Reports

Site walks, snagging and weatherproofing. Referenced findings, suggested trades, per-trade extracts and close-out tracking.

Weekly Compliance Register

Six checks, prefilled and ready to run. One non-compliant check raises an action with an owner and a due date. The run locks when it is complete, and the last six weeks sit side by side.

Custom Reports

One-off inspections, specialist audits and batch photo reviews, issued from a template rather than a blank page. Suits a bespoke check you run once and want to look the same the next time.

Three steps, start to issued.

Step one

Upload the photographs

Straight off the phone or the camera. HEIC and JPEG, with capture time and location kept intact. Full resolution originals go to the AI, display copies are handled separately.

Step two

The AI drafts the findings

Every photograph is read against the report template with structured output, confidence scoring and trade suggestions. When it is uncertain it routes to not assessed, never to a passing status.

Step three

Review and issue

Confirm or correct from the keyboard. Then issue a PDF with a contents page, per-trade extracts, close-out tracking and a share link, in the language the client needs.

Evidence and audit trail

Photograph, certificate, clause. Dated and attributable.

A finding is only useful if you can show where it came from. Every item in the report traces back to its photograph, the compliance record it belongs to, and the regulation clause it was read against.

Step one

The photograph

The full resolution original, with its capture time and location, kept as the record copy.

Step two

The certificate

The compliance record the item sits in, whether that is a register run or an issued report.

Step three

The clause

The regulation clause the item was read against, printed with the finding and dated.
